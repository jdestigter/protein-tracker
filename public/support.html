Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the protein tracking?
DailyP uses AI to estimate protein content based on your food descriptions. Estimates are approximate and intended for general wellness tracking, not as a substitute for professional dietary guidance.

How do I reset my data?

Go to Settings (gear icon in the top right) and tap "Reset Account." This will clear all your food logs and history. You can also delete and reinstall the app to start fresh.

Privacy

DailyP does not collect personal information, require an account, or show ads. All food log data is stored locally on your device. Read our full Privacy Policy.
