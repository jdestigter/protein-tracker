Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 25, 2026

DailyP ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data DailyP collects, how it is processed, and your rights.

Overview

DailyP is designed with privacy as a core principle. We do not require an account, we do not collect personal information, and all food log data is stored locally on your device. The only data transmitted from your device is the text of your food descriptions, which is sent to a third-party AI service solely to estimate protein content.

Data We Collect and Process

Food descriptions (transmitted to a third party): When you log food via voice or text input, the text of what you said or typed is sent from your device to our backend server hosted on Vercel (vercel.com), which then forwards it to Anthropic's Claude API (anthropic.com) to estimate protein content. This text may contain descriptions of food you ate, including quantities and meal details. This data is used solely to return a protein estimate to you. Our server does not log or store your food descriptions. Anthropic processes this data in accordance with their Privacy Policy and Usage Policy. Per Anthropic's API data policy, data sent through their API is not used to train their models.

Voice data: When you use voice input, speech-to-text conversion is performed entirely on-device by Apple's built-in speech recognition framework. Raw audio is never transmitted to DailyP's servers or any third party. Only the resulting text transcription is sent for protein estimation as described above.

Local data (never transmitted): Your daily protein goal, food log entries (food name, portion, protein amount), and historical tracking data are stored locally on your device using on-device storage. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to DailyP, Anthropic, or any other service.

Onboarding inputs (never transmitted): During initial setup, you may enter your weight, activity level, and fitness goal to calculate a suggested daily protein target. This information is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to any server.

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or have access to:

  • Your name, email address, or any account information (no account is required)
  • Your location or IP address
  • Health or medical data (protein estimates are for general wellness only)
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or fingerprints
  • Contacts, photos, or browsing history
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data

Third-Party Services

DailyP uses the following third-party services:

  • Anthropic Claude API (anthropic.com) — processes food description text to estimate protein content. Data sent to Anthropic consists solely of food descriptions (e.g., "two scrambled eggs and toast"). No personally identifiable information is included. Anthropic does not use API data to train models.
  • Vercel (vercel.com) — hosts the backend server that relays food descriptions to the Anthropic API. Vercel does not log or store the content of API requests made by DailyP.

DailyP does not use any other third-party services, SDKs, analytics tools, crash reporting tools, or advertising networks.

Analytics and Advertising

DailyP does not include any analytics, tracking, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs. We do not track how you use the app. We do not show ads. We have no way to know who our users are.

Data Retention

All food log data is stored locally on your device. If you delete the app or use the "Reset Account" feature in Settings, all data is permanently deleted. We have no ability to recover your data because we never had access to it. Food description text sent for protein estimation is processed in real time and is not stored by our server.

Children's Privacy

DailyP is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Health Disclaimer

DailyP provides estimated protein information for general wellness tracking purposes only. Protein estimates are approximate and may not be accurate for all foods. DailyP is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition. The protein goal suggested during onboarding is a general estimate based on commonly used formulas and is not personalized medical or nutritional advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional or registered dietitian before making changes to your diet, especially if you have any medical conditions.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact
